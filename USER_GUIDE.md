# Expense Tracker - User Guide

Welcome to your personal Expense Tracker! This guide will help you get the most out of your expense tracking experience.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Open the Application**
   - Visit the expense tracker URL in your web browser
   - The app works best in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge

2. **Install as an App (Recommended)**
   - **Desktop**: Click the install icon (⬇️) in your browser's address bar
   - **Mobile**: Tap "Add to Home Screen" from your browser menu
   - This allows you to use the app like a native mobile/desktop application

3. **Enable Notifications**
   - Click "Allow" when prompted for notification permissions
   - This enables budget alerts and payment reminders

## 💰 Managing Expenses

### Adding an Expense

1. Click the **"+ Add Expense"** button
2. Fill in the expense details:
   - **Amount**: Enter the expense amount
   - **Description**: Brief description of the expense
   - **Category**: Choose from predefined categories or create new ones
   - **Date**: Defaults to today, but you can change it
   - **Tags**: Optional tags for better organization
3. Click **"Save Expense"**

### Viewing Your Expenses

- **Recent Expenses**: See your latest expenses on the main dashboard
- **All Expenses**: Click "View All Expenses" to see your complete history
- **Filter & Search**: Use the search bar and category filters to find specific expenses

### Editing or Deleting Expenses

1. Find the expense you want to modify
2. Click the **edit (✏️)** or **delete (🗑️)** icon
3. Make your changes and save, or confirm deletion

## 📊 Budget Management

### Setting Up Your Budget

1. Go to **Settings** → **Budget Settings**
2. Set your monthly budget amount
3. Choose your budget warning threshold (default: 80%)
4. Save your settings

### Budget Alerts

You'll receive notifications when:
- You reach 80% of your monthly budget (or your custom threshold)
- You exceed your monthly budget
- Weekly spending summaries (if enabled)

### Viewing Budget Status

- **Dashboard**: See your current month's spending vs. budget
- **Analytics**: Detailed budget analysis and trends
- **Progress Bar**: Visual indicator of budget usage

## 🔔 Notification Settings

### Accessing Notification Settings

1. Click the **Settings (⚙️)** icon
2. Select **"Notification Settings"**

### Notification Types

**In-App Notifications**
- Appear within the app interface
- Always enabled for important alerts

**Push Notifications**
- Appear on your device even when the app is closed
- Require browser permission
- Can be enabled/disabled in settings

**Email Notifications** (if configured)
- Receive notifications via email
- Enter your email address in settings

### Notification Categories

**Budget Alerts**
- Threshold warnings (default: 80% of budget)
- Budget exceeded notifications
- Customizable threshold percentage

**Daily Reminders**
- Optional daily prompts to log expenses
- Set your preferred reminder time
- Can be disabled if not needed

**Weekly Summaries**
- Weekly spending overview
- Choose your preferred day and time
- Includes spending trends and insights

**Payment Reminders**
- Alerts for upcoming bill payments
- Customizable reminder timing
- Mark as paid directly from notification

### Quiet Hours

Set quiet hours to pause notifications during:
- Sleep hours (default: 10 PM - 8 AM)
- Work meetings
- Personal time

## 💳 Payment Reminders

### Creating Payment Reminders

1. Click **"Payment Reminders"** in the main menu
2. Click **"+ Create Reminder"**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Description**: What the payment is for (e.g., "Rent", "Phone Bill")
   - **Amount**: Payment amount
   - **Due Date**: When the payment is due
   - **Recurring**: Set if it repeats (monthly, weekly, etc.)
   - **Reminder Time**: How far in advance to remind you

### Managing Payment Reminders

**View All Reminders**
- See upcoming, overdue, and completed payments
- Filter by status (pending, paid, overdue)
- Sort by due date or amount

**Mark as Paid**
- Click the **"Mark as Paid"** button
- This can also be done directly from push notifications
- Automatically creates an expense entry (optional)

**Edit or Delete Reminders**
- Use the edit (✏️) or delete (🗑️) icons
- Modify recurring reminders to update future instances

### Payment Reminder Notifications

You'll receive alerts:
- 3 days before payment is due
- 1 day before payment is due
- On the due date
- Daily after the due date (until marked as paid)

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Notifications Not Working

**Check Browser Permissions**
1. Look for a notification icon in your browser's address bar
2. Click it and select "Allow notifications"
3. Refresh the page and try again

**For Chrome:**
1. Click the lock icon (🔒) in the address bar
2. Set Notifications to "Allow"
3. Refresh the page

**For Firefox:**
1. Click the shield icon in the address bar
2. Go to Permissions → Notifications → Allow

### App Not Installing

**Desktop**
- Ensure you're using HTTPS (secure connection)
- Try a different browser (Chrome works best)
- Clear browser cache and try again

**Mobile**
- Use the browser's native "Add to Home Screen" feature
- Ensure you have enough storage space
- Try closing other apps and retry

### Data Not Syncing

1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Try logging out and back in
4. Clear browser cache if issues persist

### Performance Issues

**Slow Loading**
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Close unnecessary browser tabs
- Restart your browser

**App Crashes**
- Update your browser to the latest version
- Disable browser extensions temporarily
- Try using an incognito/private window

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Expense Tracking Best Practices

1. **Log Expenses Immediately**
   - Add expenses right after making them
   - Use the mobile app for on-the-go tracking
   - Enable daily reminders if you tend to forget

2. **Use Descriptive Names**
   - "Grocery shopping at Whole Foods" vs. just "Food"
   - Include location or specific details
   - Use consistent naming for recurring expenses

3. **Categorize Consistently**
   - Stick to the same categories for similar expenses
   - Create custom categories for your specific needs
   - Review and clean up categories periodically

4. **Set Realistic Budgets**
   - Start with your actual spending patterns
   - Gradually reduce budgets to encourage savings
   - Adjust budgets seasonally if needed

### Maximizing Notifications

1. **Customize Alert Timing**
   - Set budget alerts at a threshold that gives you time to adjust
   - Schedule daily reminders for times when you typically spend
   - Use quiet hours to avoid disruptions

2. **Payment Reminder Strategy**
   - Set reminders 3-5 days before due dates
   - Use recurring reminders for regular bills
   - Mark payments as paid immediately to avoid duplicate alerts

### Analytics Insights

1. **Regular Review**
   - Check your analytics weekly
   - Look for spending patterns and trends
   - Identify your biggest expense categories

2. **Goal Setting**
   - Use analytics to set realistic savings goals
   - Track progress month over month
   - Celebrate improvements in spending habits
